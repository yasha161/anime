# 🧩 Skill 02: 2D Detective Riddle & Puzzle Engine (`viral-riddle`)

> Generate viral 2D mystery reels that hook viewers for 100% watch-time and spark thousands of comments debating the answer.

---

## 🎯 1. The Anatomy of a 10M+ View Riddle Reel

Every viral puzzle reel follows the **4-Act Formula**:

| Act | Duration | What Viewer Experiences | Visual Asset |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Act 1: The Hook** | 0s – 3s | Extreme crime scene / impossible murder setup | Yellow crime scene tape + flashing sirens |
| **Act 2: The 3 Suspects** | 3s – 12s | Suspect A, B, and C with subtle visual flaws | Character portrait cards with emojis & alibis |
| **Act 3: The 5s Countdown** | 12s – 18s | Ticking sound, heartbeat, viewer scans the frame | Animated pulsing timer bar (Red / Amber) |
| **Act 4: The Twist Reveal** | 18s – 25s | Explanation of the clue + CTA ("Did you get it?") | Magnifying glass zoom on the hidden clue |

---

## 🎨 2. Visual Style & Color Scheme

- **Background:** Deep midnight navy `#0B0F19` or crimson blood tint `#1A0B0E`.
- **Accents:** Neon Crime Caution Yellow `#FACC15` & Police Siren Red `#EF4444`.
- **Character Design:** Flat 2D vector avatars (Billionaire in suit, Maid with broom, Gardener with shears).
- **Typography:** Heavy stencil or Bebas Neue / Pragmatica bold headers for instant readability on mobile screens.
