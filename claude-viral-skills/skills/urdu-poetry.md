# ✨ Skill 03: Aesthetic Urdu Poetry & Sufi Reel Engine (`urdu-poetry`)

> Direct viral, soul-stirring vertical reels for Urdu poetry, ghazals, Sufi songs, and indie acoustic melodies (style of `@the_brave_leo`, `@qawwali.ai`, `@houseofkalaam`).

---

## 🎨 1. Aesthetic DNA & Organic Color Palette

Never use sterile, cold digital colors. Use aged, tactile South Asian materials:

- **Terracotta Courtyard Brick:** `#8B2500` / `#A0522D`
- **Velvet Crimson Rose:** `#880808` / `#990033`
- **Aged Writing Parchment:** `#FDFBF7` / `#F4ECE1`
- **Mughal Antique Brass:** `#D4AF37` / `#B45309`
- **Midnight Indigo Sky:** `#0F172A` / `#1E1B4B`

---

## 🏛️ 2. The 3 Signature Scene Archetypes

### Scene 1: Top-Down Courtyard Charpai & Rose Petal Calligraphy
- **Angle:** 90° Bird's eye flat lay view.
- **Ground:** Red herringbone brick courtyard.
- **Center:** Wooden cot (*charpai*) with ivory cotton sheet.
- **Action:** Velvety crimson rose petals drift downwards, settling naturally into the shape of Urdu letters.

### Scene 2: The Poet's Desk & Floating Manuscripts
- **Setting:** Antique dark teakwood desk by a Mughal arched window (*mehrab*) with geometric lattice (*jaali*).
- **Lighting:** Golden sunbeam rays streaming diagonally with floating dust motes.
- **Motion:** Loose parchment pages with handwritten Nastaliq verses lift into the afternoon breeze and flutter past the camera in 3D depth.

### Scene 3: The Melancholic Maiden Portrait
- **Subject:** South Asian maiden portrait with crimson dupatta softly draped.
- **Animation:** Duppata border oscillating in the breeze, subtle eye blink, and a single glistening teardrop rolling down the cheek reflecting sunlight.
