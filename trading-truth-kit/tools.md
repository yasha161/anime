# 🛠️ Official Verified Audit Tools & Free Practice Platforms

Use these legitimate, tamper-proof platforms to audit traders and practice without risking real money:

---

## 1. Verification & Auditing Tools

### [Myfxbook](https://www.myfxbook.com)
- **What it is:** The gold standard automated analytical tool for forex and CFD accounts.
- **Why it is tamper-proof:** Accounts connect directly via broker investor passwords or server APIs. Historical trades, balance curves, and drawdown are pulled straight from broker database logs.
- **What to look for:** Look for the green shields next to the account name (`Track Record Verified` + `Trading Privileges Verified`).

### [FXBlue](https://www.fxblue.com)
- **What it is:** Independent verification and trade analytics suite for MT4/MT5.
- **Best feature:** Deep statistical analysis showing risk per trade, trade duration, and Sharpe ratio.

## 2. Zero-Risk Practice Platforms

### [TradingView Paper Trading](https://www.tradingview.com)
- **Cost:** 100% Free.
- **How to activate:**
  1. Open any chart on TradingView.
  2. Click the **Trading Panel** tab at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Select **Paper Trading by TradingView** and click **Connect**.
  4. You are immediately granted \$100,000 in virtual trading capital.
- **Features:** Execute market orders, stop-losses, take-profits, and trailing stops against real-time global tick data.

### [Zerodha Varsity](https://zerodha.com/varsity)
- **Cost:** 100% Free (No account required).
- **What it is:** The highest quality free financial markets curriculum covering technical analysis, risk management, and options theory without upsells.
