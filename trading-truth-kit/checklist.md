# 📋 5-Point Fake Guru Detection Checklist

Before you follow any trading influencer, buy their course, or join their Telegram channel, run them through these 5 rapid tests:

---

### Test 1: The Bio Link Audit
- **Check:** Does their bio link point to an offshore binary options or forex broker (e.g. Quotex, Binomo, Pocket Option, Exness via affiliate sub-domain)?
- **Red Flag:** If they say *"Message me on Telegram for free VIP signals, just deposit \$100 using this broker link"*.
- **Verdict:** 100% Rev-Share scam. They get paid up to 70% when you lose that \$100.

---

### Test 2: The Screenshot Integrity Test
- **Check:** Are they posting static P&L screenshots or cropping the top header of their phone app?
- **Red Flag:** Any screenshot where the top bar of MT4/MT5 (where the account number and `[DEMO]` label appear) is cut off or covered with a sticker.
- **Verdict:** Virtual demo account pretending to be real wealth.

---

### Test 3: The Myfxbook Verification Test
- **Check:** Do they have a public, server-verified Myfxbook or FXBlue audit profile?
- **Requirements:**
  1. Both checkmarks must be green: `✓ Track Record Verified` and `✓ Trading Privileges Verified`.
  2. History must be at least 12 months long (not just 1 lucky week).
  3. Maximum drawdown must be under 25-30% (anything higher is gambling, not trading).
- **Verdict:** If they cannot provide this, their trading track record does not exist.

---

### Test 4: The 100% Win-Rate Claim
- **Check:** Do they claim "95% accuracy" or "guaranteed daily profits"?
- **Reality:** Even Renaissance Technologies (Jim Simons / Medallion Fund)—the greatest hedge fund in human history—had a win rate of approximately 50.75%. Professional hedge fund traders make their profit from risk-to-reward ratios (1:2 or 1:3), never 99% win rates.
- **Verdict:** Anyone claiming guaranteed daily profit is running a scam.

---

### Test 5: Regulatory Compliance
- **Check:** Is the broker regulated by SEBI, RBI, FCA (UK), ASIC (Australia), or CFTC (USA)?
- **Warning:** In India, trading forex pairs against non-INR currencies on unregistered offshore platforms violates FEMA guidelines (RBI Alert List).
- **Verdict:** If the broker is on the RBI Alert List, depositing money is both illegal and carries zero recovery recourse if they freeze withdrawals.
