# Digital Defense & Privacy Safety Guide 🛡️

A practical checklist to secure your browsing, subscriptions, and online identity.

---

## 1. How to Defeat Subscription Auto-Debit Scams
1. **Never use your primary physical bank debit card** for trial offers (streaming services, AI tools, SaaS apps).
2. **Use a Virtual Card** (via Privacy.com, Revolut, or your bank's virtual card feature):
   - Set the card limit to **$0 or $1**.
   - Select **Single-Use** if available (the card automatically burns itself after the trial authorization).
3. **Set a Calendar Reminder**: Always set a Google Calendar alert 48 hours before any trial expiration date.
4. If an unwanted charge does attempt to pass, the virtual card will reject it with a **"Transaction Declined: Spending Limit Exceeded"** notice.

---

## 2. Preventing Inbox Poisoning
1. **Keep 3 Email Tiers**:
   - **Tier 1 (Personal / Banking)**: Used strictly for bank accounts, government IDs, and tax portals. Never shared on social media.
   - **Tier 2 (Public / Social)**: Used for newsletters, trusted ecommerce (Amazon), and general social logins.
   - **Tier 3 (Burner / Disposable)**: Handled by **Temp-Mail** for any site requiring one-time OTP verification or downloading free assets.
2. **Use Email Aliases**: If using Gmail, append `+tag` (e.g. `yourname+trial@gmail.com`) or use iCloud Hide My Email.
